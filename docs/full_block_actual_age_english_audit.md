# Track A — raw and canonical age of ACTUAL top-selected ordinary-L columns

15 November 2026. English proof and adversarial audit prior to Lean.

## Precise statement

Fix height k, a completed old-original block prefix [0,b), the
literal finite numerical-H ordinary binary table L=finiteNumericHL(E,k,M),
and a given finite family j(t) of strictly later original names
(j(t)>=b), one per future sort. Assume E is symmetric.

Set Col_actual(z,t)=L(z,iota_k(j(t),k)), for every old numeric coordinate
z<hPosition(k,b,0), and c(i,t)=E(i,j(t)) for original i<b.

For EVERY admissible NONEMPTY support U the two raw old-age functions
agree:
  Raw_L([0,hPosition(k,b,0)), Col_actual)(U)
    = Raw_E([0,b),c)(U).
The canonical integer graph-age functions and feasible-weight families
also agree pointwise.

This is stronger than the finite table equality with a *defined*
abstract pullback column. Here the future typed column is the ACTUAL
L table against specified later top-generation vertices.

## English proof

The independently checked full-block age theorem says
  Raw_L(P, Col_pullback)=Raw_E([0,b),c)
where P is the COMPLETE numbered old prefix and Col_pullback is c on
all real generations, zero on all odd dummies.

The new actual-top column theorem (preceding stacked Track A PR #213)
shows that for every z in P, every t, BOTH ordered actual L atoms
between z and top(j(t)) equal Col_pullback(z,t). Therefore, for every
nonempty future support U, the two typed old common-neighbour pools
inside P are equal by extensionality: a vertex belongs precisely when
all its U-columns are true. Since the old binary relation L is unchanged,
these pools have identical directed clique capacity, so the raw age
functions are equal at U. Compose with the prior exact pullback-to-original
raw equality. Canonicalization and feasible vectors are functions of
the raw bound, so their equality follows by congruence.

The physical finite carrier still needs both the old-block cutoff
hPosition(k,b,0)<=M and every j(t) top-position <M.
Equality of total Boolean tables alone is NOT a statement about
finite carrier membership. No existence or scheduling of j(t) is
proved, and nothing about intermediate reserved cuts or auxiliary E/B3.

## Adversarial checklist

1. The old pool is the literal COMPLETE numbered prefix, not a
   selected active-front shadow.
2. Each future j(t) is a given original name at least b; stagewise
   existence in a fixed enumeration is a different theorem.
3. Both actual binary L directions are transported, as required by
   a symmetric graph extension, although age uses only forward bits.
4. Odd fake coordinates stay in the old pool; their true L columns
   to top selected vertices vanish, but auxiliary Kpt E may not.
5. The generator's old-real-to-top gate is open for every q<=k.
6. Complete old typed columns must be matched for EVERY t, not only
   one sort: the entire U-common-neighbour set must agree.
7. U is nonempty. The standalone full-prefix theorem relies on
   excluding odd dummies from common-neighbour sets.
8. Mixed generation cliques cannot inflate capacity: the previous
   injective projection theorem handles all generations at once.
9. Repeated future sorts or noninjective j(t) do not affect column
   equality, although they would matter for a genuine labelled
   set of distinct future vertices.
10. A clique capacity is the size of a largest clique in the OLD
    common-neighbourhood, not that neighbourhood's cardinality.
11. The raw bound may not be a genuinely positive age for an
    arbitrary column family; equality survives canonicalization.
12. All finite numeric source Boolean edges are total on Nat;
    physical carrier membership is independent and explicit.
13. This cannot replace the reserved-pivot sharp capacity
    or mixed old-neighbourhood checks at partial generation cuts.
14. Graph-age equality does NOT prove that full I1/I2/I3/B3
    dependent types are matched or that nonselected auxiliary
    interesting events are boring.
15. No unbounded coherent physical resolved-copy schedule,
    cross-occurrence labelled inverse-source orbit or BRD follows.
