22 November 2026. English proof FIRST, before Lean. Depends on G487 original-coordinate novelty interval theorem (direct gate passed, full CI pending at note creation).

## Result 1: original-coordinate trichotomy

Fix one symmetric finite numerical-H+ graph Kpt family (1,0,1), actual old AlgorithmRun R on X, nested X⊆Y, exact full target Kpt index, and a zero ordinary-L column at c∈Allowed. If c is an exact old-total-factor Bad cut, then at least one of:
(I1) some target complete Kpt source node ends at c;
(I2) two target source nodes have genuine same-component meet at c;
(O) an ACTUAL original v is ACTIVE at c, represents a new complete source crossing and has no old complete Kpt lower-socle witness at c.

In case (O), v belongs to E85's bounded maximal-prefix support of size at most |U| + |I_old(0)|. Each fixed v has an order-convex RAW source-cut novelty interval by G487.

Proof: case split on actual I1 and actual I2. If neither exists, G487's nonstructural zero-stage Bad witness gives v active and uncovered at that same cut. E86 bounds the support.

## Result 2: odd numerical-H+ cuts

At a true odd cut c=2a+1, the actual numerical-H+ ordinary-L column vanishes for EVERY true stage original by hNumericBinary_odd_left_false, as in independently verified Track A/G #481. Thus the same trichotomy and original-carrier cardinal bound hold without assuming zero stage separately. Auxiliary E still permits I1, I2 and missing old socles; oddness does NOT imply boringness.

## Result 3: bounded raw interval cover, not a Bad-count bound

For any family S of cuts where all the true zero-column, exact-Bad and no-I1/no-I2 hypotheses hold, every cut in S belongs to one of the original-coordinate novelty intervals for v in the E85 support. At most |U|+|I_old(0)| such RAW intervals suffice. Intersecting these intervals with arbitrary allowed, old-boring or zero-column masks may split them into many components. No cardinality/connected-component bound for Bad follows.
