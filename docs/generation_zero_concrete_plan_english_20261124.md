## Exact generation-zero argument

actual original v with c+1 <= freeLevel(v). The E atom at (c,v) holds.
The literal generated-E definition therefore yields v=p(j,m), m<=k,
and a generator p(i,q) with i<j, q<=k, c<=p(i,q). Necessarily b<=i
(otherwise the block-order theorem gives p(i,q)<c), hence b<j.
This derives actual later-base chronology for ALL eligible names, not just
positive L neighbours. Odd fake addresses and same-base later generations
cannot evade this argument.

The bounded role contracts classify each such name as a dummy, a selected
TOP, or a retained auxiliary. At an omitted base, a positive ordinary cut

TOP at later base j with E(b,j)=true. The cut base cannot be dummy.
Assume the following two ORDINARY-Q construction certificates:
* A positive later selected TOP at the omitted base has a genuine old
  neighbour p<b.
* On later selected TOPs, equality of complete ordinary old BASE columns
  implies equality of the b-bit.

Read the old bit at p(p,0) from equality of complete old physical types.
It is true for v. A later dummy w has negative old base edge by symmetry
and isolation; a later retained auxiliary w has negative old base edge by

The exact numerical forward relation therefore makes their old physical
bit false, a contradiction. Hence w is also a selected TOP. For every
q<b, its generation-zero coordinate lies below c and the forward ordinary
bit to TOP is exactly the base bit; equality of old physical types thus

in both orientations yields equality of both Boolean cut bits, including

gives equality at c+1. This works for all eligible stage names; no no-I2

## Concrete Plan
Define retained real positions by bounded decoding: selected coding bases
only at TOP; all generations of retained auxiliary blocks. Define Plan as
level 0, actual cap free cut, all retained real positions, and positive actual
free cuts of retained finite originals. This is the literal finite-source
version of the manuscript set (membership outside the cap is irrelevant to
a bounded run; retained real coordinates may be intersected with the cap
in the final forced-level statement). For an actual original with positive
free level, decoding gives a real positive generation. Its address cannot
be 0, and cannot be a positive odd free cut of any other original, by the
checked parity theorem. Thus if its address lies in Plan it lies in the
retained real component. Bounded injectivity proves the Plan role contract.
Its own free cut is in Plan by definition. The cap is a dummy by bounded
injectivity from its prescribed real dummy address, and its free cut lies
in Plan. No run is needed to establish these contracts. The existing
odd-cut nonselection theorem now applies with the only remaining stage
premise being descending inclusion for the given literal run.

A separate descending induction closes final inclusion whenever all local
omitted-cut nonselection implications are available: start at singleton
top, which lies in Plan. At step i either I(i)=I(i+1), or it is obtained by
inserting i. If i is omitted and inserted, the verified final-tail identity
puts i in I(0), contradicting the local implication with the already proved
I(i+1) subset Plan. Thus all stages are contained in Plan.
