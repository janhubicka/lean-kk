## Theorem E88.1 — obstruction dichotomy from failure of synchronized run existence

Assume there is **NO** AlgorithmRun RY on Y of the same top with literal

    RY.I = R.I AND RY.F = R.F.

Then at least one of the following is true:

(A) FailedPrefix is nonempty (one ACTUAL physical original v has no OLD-LIVE complete Kpt ancestor mate at its HIGHEST eligible Allowed old-boring source cut).

(B) There is a genuinely OLD-BORING physical cut c OUTSIDE Allowed where full parameter-closed physical no-novelty fails.

### English proof

Argue by contraposition. If (A) fails, every v with S_v nonempty has an old source prefix witness at max S_v, since v then belongs to Need and the definition of FailedPrefix contains every failure. This is precisely E84's hMax premise.

If (B) fails, for every c<top outside Allowed that is genuinely boring on the old full Kpt source, physical NewRootNoNoveltyAtCut holds. This is precisely E84's hOtherQuiet premise.

The physical Allowed safe-class hypothesis and one-bare-root source data are unchanged. Hence the fully verified E84 construction produces an ACTUAL new AlgorithmRun with exactly old I and TOTAL F, contrary to nonexistence. So (A) or (B) must hold.

This use of E84 is genuinely contrapositive; **a failed prefix certificate by itself does NOT show a synchronized run cannot exist**, because E84 is sufficient, not necessary. Likewise an outside-Allowed no-novelty failure may have a different new-run selected schedule; it does not identify the final BRD obstruction.

## Theorem E88.2 — if other old-boring cuts are quiet, a concrete original witness fails

Under the same data and nonexistence of a synchronized Y-run, suppose the complete physical no-novelty hypothesis is independently verified for EVERY genuinely OLD-BORING cut outside Allowed.

Then FailedPrefix is nonempty. Choose one failing v and its true nonempty S_v. Thus the defect is a failure of an ACTUAL complete old-Kpt prefix at the HIGHEST eligible Allowed cut, not an invented target graph-age type.

E79's downward monotonicity implies that if the old prefix is absent at some lower eligible cut then it is absent at the maximal cut; this is why the maximal test is a valid obstruction location. The theorem does not say every lower cut fails.
