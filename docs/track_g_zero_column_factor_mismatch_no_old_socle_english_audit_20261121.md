## Theorem

There cannot exist ANY old live source Kpt node w∈closure(S,RX.I(c+1),X), crossing c, with the SAME COMPLETE old Kpt ancestor z|c=w|c. This is stronger than the generic Track G factor-mismatch witness theorem, which only excludes an identical entire paired old/upper ancestor record.

## English proof

Suppose such a w exists. E72's independently verified `finiteNumericH_candidate_zero_column_old_socle_upper_covered` applies because the actual stage column is zero for all eligible originals, z is in the complete target closure, w is in the complete old closure, both cross c, and their COMPLETE old Kpt ancestors are literally equal. It yields one old live witness w' with the SAME UPPER ancestor z|(c+1)=w'|(c+1). The verified restriction theorem `kpt_LevelTree_lower_ancestors_eq_of_higher_eq` forces their lower ancestors to coincide as well. Hence w' realizes the exact same complete old/upper ancestor PAIR in the old source. But G432's verified `kpt_old_boring_factor_mismatch_has_no_old_full_pair` excludes precisely that situation for the total old F-step factor D. Contradiction.

No equality of source sets, no chosen Y AlgorithmRun, no finite candidate source index, no no-I2 assumption and no ordinary-L reduct matching is used. The complete dependent Kpt types include auxiliary E and negative ordinary bits.

## Adversarial checks

2. The zero ordinary-L hypothesis quantifies over ALL eligible true stage originals, not only z's representative.
3. The full old Kpt ancestor equality includes dependent E; an ordinary graph-age equality is too weak.
4. A genuine old AlgorithmRun is required, and the cut must be old boring; otherwise the old skip-factor crossing law need not exist.
5. Total F-step factorization makes D unique by left cancellation. It is not a finite graph automorphism.
6. The proof uses the complete actual candidate and old parameter/meet closures at RX.I(c+1).
7. E72's upper cover is an existential old live w' possibly different from w; that is sufficient.
8. Equality of upper ancestors implies equality of lower ancestors by the actual LevelTree restriction law, not source-prefix closure.
9. The physical node z may be a newly activated old-final name; absence of an old LIVE socle witness does not imply new-final status.
10. A mismatch at a zero stage therefore points to a genuinely missing OLD full-socle ancestor; it is not merely a new upper L+/E continuation.
11. K4 genuine auxiliary cuts 10 and 22 cannot be declared boring or zero without verifying the hypotheses.
12. This is a one-cut source-local theorem; it does not bound the number of missing old socles, control terminals or I2 meet pairs, construct the resolved unbounded H+ copy or prove sharp BRD counts.
