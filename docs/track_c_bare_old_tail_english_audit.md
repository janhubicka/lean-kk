**15 November 2026. English first, before Lean.** Scope: one-step numerical `H^+` source, not the global resolved diary.

## Mathematical issue found in the prior audit

Track C's verified `finiteNumericH_newRoot_preCut_absorbs_old_run_larger_top` requires
`∀ q∈Rold.I 0, cut<q`. This can be **vacuous** when the old run selects 0 (indeed whenever it selects any level ≤cut). It is not a proof that lower old selected parameter levels can be omitted from complete source closure. In the older Yfull construction, inherited `closure_(Rold.I0)(X)` may have produced low dependent nodes that a genuinely bare new-root run would not regenerate before its level becomes selected. Direct full-closure equality does not follow from selected-level monotonicity.

The revised source presentation for the physical next stage is **BARE**:
`Ybare = X ∪ {one actual new admissible numerical-H Kpt ORIGINAL}`,
where `X` is the true old initial Kpt seed set. It does *not* insert the new original's ancestor prefix pool or preinsert `closure_(Rold.I0) X`. `Ybare` is a subset of any full inherited pool but the converse is not assumed.

## New exact English theorem — nonvacuous old-tail retention

Let `H` be a genuine successor tree, let `Rold` be a genuine AlgorithmRun on `X` with scan top `t`, and `Rnew` a genuine AlgorithmRun on `Y⊇X` with top `T≥t`. Suppose **one actual old seed** `x∈X` has level `t`. Then for every `c<t` and every old selected level `q∈Rold.I(0)` with `c<q`,
`q∈Rnew.I(c+1)`.

**Proof.** Track C's already exact-head checked cross-height monotonicity
`envelope_algorithmRun_selected_levels_mono_seed_height` gives
`Rold.I(c+1) ⊆ Rnew.I(c+1)`, since `c+1≤t`. The genuine old AlgorithmRun final-tail theorem `Rold.I_eq_final_tail_of_run` states
`Rold.I(c+1) = {q∈Rold.I(0): c+1≤q}`. Therefore an old selected `q>c` belongs to the left set, hence to the right. **No assumption that all old selected levels are above c is used.**

For a numerical-H bare-root source, `Ybare` *definitionally contains* `X`, even when the new root is at a taller physical free cut. The generic theorem applies to the same underlying `normalizedKptSMTree family`; it yields the conclusion for `Ybare` with the actual physically admissible original and without inserting any of its ancestors into the initial seeds.

## Deliberately NOT claimed
- We do not prove `closure_(Rold.I0) X ⊆ closure_(Rnew.I(c+1)) Ybare`: low old parameter-generating levels `q≤c` are **not** covered by the tail inclusion.
- We do not prove equality of OLD/NEW complete source closure sets, of full dependent `L^+/E` records or of corrected (B3) tests at any cut. A lower-level parameter might alter a source record unless physically classified.
- We do not prove that any extra selected level is an allowed resolved auxiliary microevent. In K4, genuine q2 auxiliary cuts 10 and 22 remain real.
- We do not identify source coordinates across two different numerical-H original base models or two unrelated diary occurrences; both runs use the SAME actual successor tree.
- We do not conclude a uniform finite source, labelled inverse orbit, or BRD upper bound.

## Adversarial cases
1. Old `I0` contains 0; the theorem still applies for all higher selected `q>c`, whereas the old all-above hypothesis fails.
2. A source `q=c` is deliberately excluded; selected levels at the exact tested cut may affect I1 directly.
3. A source `q<c` is excluded; no empty-tail equality of full closures is inferred.
4. Distinct scan tops are allowed, provided `oldTop≤newTop`.
5. A phantom old top (no real old top seed) is not allowed, because the old top may be skipped in the taller run.
6. New bare seeds may create additional I1/I2/I3 events. Only **inclusion**, not equality, is proved.
7. The new root is a singleton *original Kpt node*, not its ancestor prefix set.
8. The new physical structure is required to avoid the actual normalized forbidden family to make its Kpt original admissible.
9. The proof never converts `L` selected-front age agreement into full `L^+/E` closure agreement.
10. No generic `n`-type or fully labelled monoid orbit is inferred.
