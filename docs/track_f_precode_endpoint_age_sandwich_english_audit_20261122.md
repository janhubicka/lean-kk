## Correct finite statement and what it does not assume

Fix a genuine target ordinary-edge table E, any ell≥2, and a finite family of sorts Fin(d) with a distinguished coded sort j. Let old, pre, far be **genuine** families of Henson one-types at common levels m≤c≤H, with old_i≤pre_i≤far_i for every sort including the coded one. The latter coordinate of far is permitted to be a DUMMY continuation of the coded pre-type; it is NOT asserted to correspond to an actual future selected coding branch. In the application one constructs it by rank-preserving zero padding of pre_j to H, while far_i (i≠j) are the actual synchronized surviving types.

Let F, P, G be the COMPLETE normalized integer-witness canonical ages of old, pre, far, respectively, computed from the physical true common-old-neighbour clique capacities on EVERY admissible nonempty support. Suppose the later class has the same values as the old class on EVERY support **not containing j**:
`G(T)=F(T) whenever j∉T`.
This is a concrete endpoint-age equality, not an assumption about P, and must eventually be derived from the verified source→target finite class comparison together with the full canonicalizer’s locality under deletion.

## Lemma A: actual survivor-support sandwich

Genuine chronological extension and verified complete physical canonical-age antitonicity (#438) give
`G(T)≤P(T)≤F(T)`
for ALL admissible T. For a survivor support T with j∉T, the endpoint hypothesis gives G(T)=F(T). Antisymmetry forces P(T)=F(T) on ALL such supports, including any triple entirely contained in survivors. No assumption of P-survivor agreement is made: it is DERIVED from genuine endpoint data.

This is true for arbitrary ell and d; if d=1 or j is the only sort, the survivor assertions are vacuous and no empty support is invented. The coded dummy far_j is harmless provided it is a genuine chronological continuation, because the argument only uses the full age and end-value hypothesis on survivor supports.

## Lemma B: full pre-code rigidity from endpoint data and legal zero-code

Additionally require (1) P({j})=F({j})=1 and (2) the legal zero-code law of the prescribed F on EVERY mixed support containing j:
`F(T)=F(T.erase j)` whenever T contains j and T.erase j is a NONEMPTY admissible support.

Thus the true pre-code front cannot undergo a hidden strict age decrease before the independent-code event **once** the later endpoint survivor agreement has been established.
