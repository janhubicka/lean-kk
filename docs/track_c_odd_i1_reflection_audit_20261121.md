## Proof (English)

The genuine physical root free level is newTop, and the verified
old-top source geometry gives oldTop≤newTop. Thus for c<oldTop
the newly inserted original O_new cannot end at c.

Suppose the old source has an INITIAL seed x∈X of level c.
Then x belongs to the old pre-cut closure because closures include
their literal seed sets. Hence the old source has an I1 terminal,
so the desired implication follows regardless of a new terminal.

Otherwise no old initial seed ends at c. Since O_new also does not
end at c, no seed of Y ends at c. The verified finite final original
support lemma says c∉F forces NO actual original coordinate of the
entire W-stage original pool to have E-free level c, with W the
physically constructed root-prefix pool. The independently checked
Track D odd no-full-I1 theorem therefore excludes ALL generated
I1 terminals in the candidate closure, including selected E
parameters and meets. Thus the premise of a new I1 terminal is
impossible. The two cases exhaust the possibilities.

The result does not need any old-prefix witness because I1
reflection is a strictly weaker statement than full physical
`NewRootNoNoveltyAtCut` (which also requires I3 same-node old/upper
crossing-pair inclusion).
